FAQ

Quick Answers to Your Questions

Do you support custom product development?

Yes, we offer complete product customization services. We can customize output voltage, current, connector specifications, enclosure dimensions and more based on client requirements. The customization process includes four stages: requirements consultation, solution design, sample development, and mass production. Minimum order quantities vary by specification — please contact us for details.

What is the typical lead time for orders?

Standard products: in-stock items ship within 24 hours; non-stock items are delivered within 7–15 business days. Custom products: sample development takes approximately 10–20 business days; mass production lead time depends on order quantity, typically 15–30 business days. Our production management system ensures on-time delivery.

How is product quality assured?

We strictly follow the ISO 9001 quality management system. All products pass international certifications including CE, FCC, and RoHS. Before leaving the factory, every product undergoes rigorous electrical performance testing, aging tests, and EMC testing. We offer a warranty period — products damaged due to non-human factors will be replaced or repaired free of charge.

Do you provide technical support?

We have a professional technical support team providing full-process support services, including: product selection guidance, application solution design, circuit optimization advice, and troubleshooting. Our technical support hotline and email are responsive during business hours to ensure client issues are resolved promptly.
