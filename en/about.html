Company Profile

Feisheng Technology Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2009, Feisheng Technology is a high-tech enterprise specializing in the R&D, design, and manufacturing of power supply products. Our headquarters is located in Bao'an District, Shenzhen, with a modern production facility and R&D laboratory.

As a professional power supply solution provider, we are dedicated to delivering high-quality, highly reliable power products for consumer electronics, communications equipment, and industrial control sectors. With years of industry experience and deep technical expertise, we have grown into one of the leading power supply solution providers in the industry.
